# Research agenda

## Central question
**How sensitive are shortest-path decisions to incident severity and incomplete/late disruption information?**

## Hypotheses
### H1
Moderate incident penalties can reroute traffic before full closure occurs.

### H2
Delayed incident information increases realized travel time relative to perfect-information routing.

### H3
Networks with few alternate paths are disproportionately sensitive to single-edge disruption.

## Experiments
1. Sweep incident penalties on critical and noncritical edges.
2. Simulate incident-information delays and compare realized vs predicted path time.
3. Generate networks with different redundancy and measure disruption resilience.

## Metrics
- travel time
- reroute frequency
- delay regret
- network resilience
- path overlap before/after incident
